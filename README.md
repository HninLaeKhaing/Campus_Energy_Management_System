# ⚡ EduEnergy — Campus Energy Management System

**EduEnergy** is a web-based Campus Energy Management System designed to monitor and analyze energy consumption across campus buildings and departments. The project follows the **Design Thinking approach** to address energy-efficiency and sustainability challenges in educational institutions.

## 🚀 Features

* 📊 Real-time energy dashboard
* 🏢 Building-wise energy consumption
* 📈 Weekly consumption analytics
* 🏫 Department usage visualization
* 🚨 Energy usage alerts
* 🎛️ Smart lighting, HVAC and plug controls
* 🌱 Carbon footprint calculation
* 💡 Energy-saving suggestions
* ⭐ Sustainability score

## 🛠️ Technologies

* **React.js**
* **JavaScript**
* **HTML & CSS**
* **Tailwind CSS**
* **Recharts** — Data visualization
* **Lucide React** — Icons

## 💡 Design Thinking

The project follows:

**Empathize → Define → Ideate → Prototype → Test**

It focuses on helping campus administrators identify energy waste, monitor consumption, and make better energy-management decisions.

## ⚡ Energy & Sustainability

The system provides:

* Energy consumption monitoring
* Carbon-footprint estimation
* Renewable energy source comparison
* Energy-saving recommendations
* Building efficiency analysis

The project is aligned with the broader goals of **energy conservation, sustainable buildings, and energy efficiency**, with relevant Indian frameworks such as BEE's building-energy guidelines considered during the design.

> The current dashboard uses prototype/demo data and can be connected to real-time IoT energy meters in future.
